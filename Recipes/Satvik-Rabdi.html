Satvik-Rabdi

DISCRIPTION

Satvik Rabdi is a healthy, vegan, sugar-free, and dairy free adaptation of the classic Indian sweet Dessert.

INGREDIENTS

  • 20 soaked cashews
  • 1/2 cup chopped sweeet potato
  • 1/2 + 1/2 cup water
  • 3 tablespoons jaggery powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon cardamom powder
  • 25 saffron strands, soaked
  • pinch of rock salt

Topping

  • 5 almond, roasted and chopped
  • 5 pistachios, roasted and chopped
  • 1 teaspoon rose petal (optional)

PREPARATION

  • Soak 20 cashews in water for about 6 hours.
  • Soak 25 saffron stands in 1 tablespoons of warm water for 2 hours.

METHOD

  1. Blend the cashew, raw sweet potato, and 1/2 cup of water in a blender until creamy.
  2. Heat a saucepan over low flame. Pour in the remaining 1/2 cup of water and the blended mixture. Gradually increase the flame to medium while stirring continuously.
  3. When the mixture starts to boil,cook it for 10-15 minutes on low flame.
  4. Add the jaggery and cook for 3 more minutes
  5. Switch off the flame, add the cardamom powder, soaked saffron along with the water in which it was soaked, and salt. Mix well
  6. To serve warm, immediately add the Toppings and server. To serve cold, refrigerate for 1 hour, then add the toppings and serve.
