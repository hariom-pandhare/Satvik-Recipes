Coconut Ladoo

DESCRIPTION

Coconut Ladoos are healthy plant based, and sugar free sweet spheres made from natural ingredients like grated coconut, cashews and raisins

INGREDIENTS

  • 1/4 cup cashews, soaked
  • 1/2 cup raisins
  • 1/2 cup grated dry coconut (gola)

Coating

  • 1/4 cup finely grated dry coconut

PREPARATION

  • Soak 1/4 cup cashews in water for about 6 hours.

METHOD

  1. Place all the ingredients, except the coating, into a blender and blend.
  2. Shape the mixture into small balls and coat them with grated gola
  3. The ladoos can be served immediately or stored for 3-4 days in the refrigerator.
