Dahi Vada

DESCRIPTION

Dahi Vada is the healthy Indian recipe, this recipe is steamed instead of frying in refined oil and is made with natural ingredients

INGREDIENTS

Vada

  • 1 cup soaked whole urad daal (with skin)
  • 2 small green chilis, finely chopped
  • 1 inch ginger, grated
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 1/2 teaspoon rock salt

Flavoured Dahi

  • 3 cups peanut curd
  • 3 tablespoons lemon juice
  • 1/4 cup jaggery powder
  • tablespoons roasted cumin powder
  • teaspoon black salt

Assembly

  • 1 & 1/2 cups date chutney
  • 1 & 1/2 cups green chutney
  • 1/4 cup pomegranate
  • 1/2 cup chopped coriander

PREPARATION

  • Soak 1 cup whole urad daal for about 6 hours.
  • Blend the soaked urad daal until smooth. Then place it in a bowl, cover with a plate, and put it to ferment for 8-10 hours
  • Prepare 3 cups peanut curd
  • Prepare 1 & 1/2 cups date chutney
  • Prepare 1 & 1/2 cups green chutney

METHOD

  1. Crush the green chili, ginger, cumin seeds, and salt in a mortar and pestle.
  2. Add the crushed spices to the fermented vada batter and mix well using a spoon.
  3. Grease the plates of an idli steamer with a little water. Place 2 tablespoons of the vada batter on each idli mold and steam fo 20 mins on low flame.
  4. For the flavoured dahi, place all its ingredients in a bowl and stir well. Refrigerate for 30 minutes.
  5. Check the vadas by inserting a fork. If the batter sticks to the fork, steam for a few more minutes.
  6. Remove the vadas while still warm. Soak them in warm water for 10 mins.
  7. Squeeze out the water from vadas and add them to the flavoured dahi bowl. Refrigerate for 30 mins.
  8. When ready to serve, place the vada in serving bowls and add all the assembly ingredients on top.
